ENTERPRISE AI CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY 2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. 
Employees with more than 5 years of tenure receive an additional 5 days (total 25 days).
Leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually. 
A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Unused sick leave does not roll over to the next year.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave.
This applies to both birth and adoption.

4. SABBATICAL
After 7 continuous years of service, employees are eligible for a 6-week paid sabbatical to pursue personal or professional development.

5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
The company observes all federal holidays. If a holiday falls on a weekend, the preceding Friday or following Monday will be observed as a day off.
