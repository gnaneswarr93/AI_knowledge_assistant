ENTERPRISE AI CORPORATION - Q3 2026 FINANCIAL SUMMARY
Date: October 15, 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The third quarter of 2026 demonstrated strong growth for Enterprise AI Corp, driven by the successful launch of our Multi-Agent AI orchestration platform.

REVENUE
Total Q3 Revenue reached $45.2 million, representing a 28% increase year-over-year.
- Software Subscriptions: $32.4 million
- Professional Services: $10.1 million
- Hardware/Infrastructure: $2.7 million

OPERATING EXPENSES
Total operating expenses were $29.8 million.
- Research & Development (R&D): $14.5 million (significant investment in vector database scaling)
- Sales & Marketing: $9.2 million
- General & Administrative: $6.1 million

NET PROFIT
Net profit for Q3 was $11.4 million (25.2% profit margin), exceeding our initial guidance of $9 million.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS
We onboarded 42 new enterprise clients this quarter. The customer churn rate remained exceptionally low at 1.2%.

FUTURE OUTLOOK (Q4 2026)
We project Q4 revenue to reach $50 million. Our primary focus will be expanding our data center footprint in Europe and launching the new generative voice analytics suite.
